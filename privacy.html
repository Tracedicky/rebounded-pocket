Privacy policy: Rebounded: Pocket Edition

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Rebounded: Pocket Edition ("the game") is made by AVENGIS, a solo developer. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game does not collect personal information, has no accounts, shows no ads, and does not track you.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, records, medals, wardrobe, painted decks and settings are saved on your phone only. They are never sent to AVENGIS or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online Versus

If you choose to play online Versus, the game connects to a small room server run by AVENGIS on Cloudflare, so that two phones can play the same match. While you play, the server passes between the two phones only what the match needs: a short room code, each player's moves, the heroes picked, and the wardrobe pieces each player is wearing.

The server does not ask for your name, does not keep a record of matches, and does not store what passes through it. A room closes when the match ends, or after three hours at most. Like any service on the internet, Cloudflare handles your phone's internet address to make the connection; see Cloudflare's own privacy policy for how it treats network data. Playing online is optional: every other mode works without an internet connection.

Purchases

If the game offers a purchase, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. AVENGIS never sees your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock what you bought.

Permissions

The game asks for internet access (for online Versus) and vibration (for the optional rumble, which can be switched off). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
