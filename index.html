TESTERS WANTED

Rebounded: Pocket Edition is coming to Android, and I need players to test it before it launches. It's free.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Press JOIN THE TEST. It opens Google's group page: press Join group, signed in with the Google account on your Android phone.
    Heads up: Google may first say "You don't have permission to access this content". Nothing's wrong, that's just Google's page for people who haven't joined yet. Tap the blue joining the group link under it, then Join group.
  2. The test is open! Once you've joined the group, open the test's page on Google Play on your Android phone, press Become a tester, then install the game from the Play Store.
    (It can take a few minutes after joining the group before Google lets you in. If the Play Store says "Item not found", make sure it's signed in with the same Google account, then clear its cache: Settings, Apps, Google Play Store, Storage, Clear cache. Then open the link again.)
  3. Keep it installed for 14 days. Play when you like, and tell me what you think, good or bad.

Why 14 days? Google won't let a new developer release a game until at least 12 testers have had it for 14 days, so every person who joins really counts.

THE GAME

A pixel-art brick breaker where the water rises and falls in every stage, and your deck rides it. Pick one of five heroes, each with a power of their own. Five sectors to restore, each ending with a living warden, plus THE FLOOD, Endless, Versus on one phone or online, and a deck painter.
